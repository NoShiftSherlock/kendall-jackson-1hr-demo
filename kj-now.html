About KJ Now orders

KJ Now orders are fulfilled and sold by licensed third-party retailers in your area, not by the Kendall-Jackson winery. That is what makes one-hour delivery possible.

Because these are retail sales, kj.com promotions do not apply. The 20% off four or more bottles offer and Club '82 pricing are available on standard shipping orders at kj.com.

Your account and order history

KJ Now checkout is separate from your kj.com account, so these orders will not appear in your kj.com order history.

Questions about a KJ Now delivery? Email ondemand@kj.com and our team will track it down, usually within two hours. For shipped orders, contact kj.com customer care as usual.
